HOW TO PLAY

Choose your faction on the opening screen. Start with 3 cards; your hand can grow without limit. Each deck contains 20 cards, with at most 4 copies of a card. Drag a card from your hand onto an empty friendly lane. Drag an Elf on your field into an adjacent empty lane to move it, or drag one friendly creature onto another to fuse. Drag equipment onto any adventurer, or a Potion onto an injured ally. Drag environments onto the shared environment slot; Dungeon rewards its owner once per turn when a friendly creature is defeated: draw a card or give a random surviving ally +1 permanent ATK. Each new environment replaces the previous one for BOTH sides. A coin flip decides the first player. The first player cannot attack on the opening turn; afterward newly played creatures can attack immediately. Click your ready creature and then an enemy card to attack. Click an EMPTY enemy lane in range to attack the opponent's hearts (only if no enemy is within your attack range). You can also click the hearts directly. Click END TURN when ready. On phones, tap a card and then tap its destination if dragging is difficult.
